02 — Why modular

Why an AvaHome modular home?

Modular construction removes the uncertainty of a traditional building site.

Off-grid ready

Solar-powered configurations keep you independent of the grid, wherever you are — with the option to connect to an external network.

Controlled quality

Every module is built indoors, protected from the weather, with quality checks at every stage.

Predictable cost

The price is fixed from the start — none of the overruns and surprises of a conventional build.

Energy efficient

High-performance insulation, multi-layer glazing and low running costs all year round.

Expandable

Need more space in a few years? Add new modules without rebuilding what you have.

Goes anywhere

Modules reach the mountains, the lakeside or the city. The House Boat is delivered straight to the water.
