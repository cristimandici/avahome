House Boat

Wake up with the water at your feet. The House Boat is a floating home built modular on a pontoon structure — stable, insulated and fully equipped. Perfect for a lake, a lagoon or a river delta.

The concept

Premium houseboat with extra space and a modern aesthetic design

A unique concept in home ownership, floating homes are comfortable and built on the water. These exclusive developments are perfect for a downsizing move or a holiday home, where the setting and the lifestyle are part of the package.

01

Living room

The living room is designed for a maximum experience in contact with nature. The glass walls place you directly in the natural environment where you navigate.

You will be able to admire the sunrises and sunsets directly from the middle of the waters, enjoying comfort in a warm and romantic interior.

02

Kitchen

There is also a pantry equipped with cooking tools, a sink, a countertop and an integrated refrigerator.

Compact, with generous dimensions, it offers you absolutely everything you need to sail in a romantic setting with your partner.

03

Bedroom

The bedroom offers enough space for a couple. The design as well as its location offer you privacy, while the boat glides along the waters you have chosen to sail.

You can experience a relaxing vacation while making memories that will last a lifetime.

Life on the water, without compromise

House Boat modules are built in our workshop and assembled on a floating pontoon engineered for maximum stability. The home is delivered straight to the water — and it can be relocated later, an advantage no shoreline build can match.

  • Floating pontoon structure — stable and durable
  • Modular build, configured around your needs
  • Deck above the water with safety railing
  • Large glazing facing the water, high-performance frames
  • Insulated for year-round use, winter included
  • Complete installations: electrical, plumbing, optional solar
  • Relocatable — move it to a new spot on the water
  • Ideal as a holiday home or rental unit (business)
