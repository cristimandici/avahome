Movable Cottage

A home that doesn't compete with the landscape — it reflects it. The secured mirror facade makes the Movable Cottage melt into its surroundings, while the interior stays warm, bright and insulated for all four seasons.

The concept

Premium house with extra space and a modern aesthetic design

The living space is optimally thought out. Everything that matters is at hand. The home is generous, the placement of the furniture, according to a modern design, is comforting. Light colors with a neutral tone, of a pleasant shade will relax you.

The Movable Cottage is independent of electricity. The power source, with solar panels, ensures the consumption of the necessary electrical energy that you need regardless of where you are and how far you are from civilization. If you wish, there is also the possibility of connecting to an external electricity network.

01

Living room

The living room is the main place where you spend your time. It has a perfectly functional fireplace for heating the cool evenings.

The generous opening of the sliding door allows you to admire a sunrise or sunset in a fabulous natural setting.

02

Kitchen

The kitchen is compact — not a generous space, but it offers you everything you need for the vacation you spend in nature.

We paid attention to details, knowing that vacation time does not mean time spent in a sophisticated, high-cost kitchen.

03

Bedroom

The optimally organized bedroom offers comfort for everyone's taste. Due to its openness, it gives the impression of outer space.

The window located above the bed offers optimal lighting and lets you watch the starry sky on clear nights.

Bathroom

Design that disappears into the landscape

Built from prefabricated modules, the Movable Cottage installs on almost any site: a clearing, a forest edge, a hillside or your own backyard. The exterior mirror panels are toughened, weather-resistant and bird-safe.

  • Secured mirror-panel facade, built for the outdoors
  • Modular frame structure — extend it later with new modules
  • Thermal insulation for permanent, four-season living
  • Generous triple glazing with high-performance frames
  • Complete electrical and plumbing installations, factory-fitted
  • Interior finishes of your choice — natural wood, warm tones
  • Treated-wood access deck included
  • Movable — relocate it to a new site whenever you need
