Movable Cottage

A home that doesn't compete with the landscape — it reflects it. The secured mirror facade makes the Movable Cottage melt into its surroundings, while the interior stays warm, bright and insulated for all four seasons. And when you move on, it moves with you.

Design that disappears into the landscape

Built from prefabricated modules, the Movable Cottage installs in days on almost any site: a clearing, a forest edge, a hillside or your own backyard. The exterior mirror panels are toughened, weather-resistant and bird-safe.

  • Secured mirror-panel facade, built for the outdoors
  • Modular frame structure — extend it later with new modules
  • Thermal insulation for permanent, four-season living
  • Generous triple glazing with high-performance frames
  • Complete electrical and plumbing installations, factory-fitted
  • Interior finishes of your choice — natural wood, warm tones
  • Treated-wood access deck included
  • Movable — relocate it to a new site whenever you need
